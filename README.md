# Green

A green stick figure who walks back and forth along the bottom of your screen,
like the ones in Alan Becker's animations. He has no face, and your mouse goes
straight through him.

Every so often **the Chosen One**, the black stick figure, walks in from the side.
Green sees him coming and stops. They fight. The Chosen One wins, cheers, and
walks home. A little later Green comes back and starts walking again.

## Start him

```
./build.sh
open Green.app
```

A little **Green** shows up in the menu bar at the top. Click it and pick **Quit Green** to stop him.

Made by Elduin. Mac only. A fan project, not made by or connected to Alan Becker.
